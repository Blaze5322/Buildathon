02  Explore themes

Innovate where it matters most

The Buildathon invites participants to develop AI solutions that address real-world challenges across diverse sectors. Each theme reflects Mozilla’s vision of building human-centred, inclusive and community-driven technology that creates meaningful impact.

01

Education

Reimagine learning through AI-powered solutions that make education more accessible, engaging and inclusive.

 02

Healthcare

Improve access to reliable health information and public health communication through responsible AI.

 03

Agriculture

Support farmers and rural communities with AI solutions that enhance agricultural practices and improve productivity.

 04

Climate

Develop technologies that help communities respond to environmental challenges and build climate resilience.

 05

Accessibility

Design inclusive AI experiences that empower people of all abilities.

 06

Civic Services

Make public services and welfare information easier to access and understand.

 07

Worker Rights

Build AI tools that support workers through practical, inclusive and community-focused solutions.
